# DOMINOVA Hero Video

Place your cinematic hero video here:
`dominova-hero.mp4`

The video should be:
- Format: MP4 (H.264 recommended)
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or fullscreen
- Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or higher

The video architecture is ready. Drop in `dominova-hero.mp4` and the hero will automatically display it.

Video playback is controlled by GSAP ScrollTrigger (currentTime scrub).
